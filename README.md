# 🌿 HERBALANCE AI

### AI-Powered Women's Health & Wellness Companion

HERBALANCE AI is an intelligent women's health and wellness application designed to provide personalized, accessible, and AI-powered guidance for everyday health and wellness needs.

The application combines Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and personalized recommendations to create a unified digital companion for women's healthcare and wellness.

---

## 🌸 Problem Statement

Women often need to manage different aspects of their health and wellness separately, including skincare, menstrual health, medicines, and personal health records.

Many existing solutions provide generic information rather than personalized guidance.

HERBALANCE AI aims to bring these services together in a single intelligent platform that provides personalized and easy-to-understand recommendations.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

HERBALANCE AI provides an AI-powered platform that can assist users with:

- 🌸 Personalized skincare analysis
- 💄 Cosmetic recommendations
- 💊 Medicine information and checking
- 🩸 Menstrual cycle tracking and prediction
- 🔐 Secure health record management
- 🤖 AI-powered health and wellness assistance

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧴 Skin Analysis
Analyze skin characteristics using Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to provide personalized skincare insights.

### 💄 Cosmetic Recommendation
Recommend suitable cosmetic products based on the user's skin characteristics and preferences.

### 💊 Medicine Checking
Provide medicine-related information and help users understand medicines and their usage.

> **Note:** HERBALANCE AI is intended to provide informational guidance and does not replace professional medical advice.

### 🩸 Menstrual Cycle Prediction
Use user-provided menstrual cycle information to estimate upcoming periods and support menstrual health tracking.

### 🔐 Secure Health Records
Provide a secure space for users to manage their personal health-related information.

### 🤖 AI Health Assistant
An intelligent assistant designed to provide simple and personalized wellness guidance.
